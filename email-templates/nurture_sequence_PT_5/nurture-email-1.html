Put it on the graphic

Hi {$name} 👋

You're running an offer this week and the only place it exists is in your head.

So you post a photo of the thing, write a caption about how great it is, and wait for someone to DM you asking how much. Nobody does. They scroll. Not because they weren't interested, but because working out whether they could afford it was too much effort at 8pm on a Tuesday.

Prompt 14 turns that into a graphic that does the work for you.

Three levels of text and no more. The offer is the biggest thing on there. The price is second. Everything else is small. And the price has to survive being shrunk to the size of a stamp, because that's roughly how big it is in a feed.

That's the rule most people get wrong. They make the business name the hero. Nobody is deciding based on your business name. They're deciding on what it is and what it costs.

No starbursts. No fake price tags. No clip-art discount stickers. Your colours, your fonts, plenty of empty space, because crowded reads cheap.
