No pitch today. Just one before-and-after from our own kitchen, because it explains the AI thing better than any explanation could.

Before

A weekly content plan for a client (7 posts, hooks, captions, formats) took about 7–8 hours. Research, drafting, rewriting, formatting. A full working day, most weeks.

After

The same plan now takes about 90 minutes. AI does the research and first drafts through a prompt system we built. A human (usually me or Agata) does the judgment: killing weak ideas, sharpening hooks, matching brand voice.

The plan is better than it used to be, because the thinking gets the time instead of the typing.

That's the whole model. AI for volume, humans for judgment. Not AI instead of people. AI so the people do the part that matters.
